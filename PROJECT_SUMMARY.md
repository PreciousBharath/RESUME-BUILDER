```bash

```

```bash
python app.py
```

### Step 3: Open in Browser
```
http://localhost:5000
```

### Step 4: Create Your Resume
1. Click "Start Building"
2. Fill in your information
3. Upload a photo (optional)
4. Preview in real-time
5. Choose template & format
6. Download!

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Best Results:
1. **Use professional photo** - High quality, clear headshot
2. **Format skills properly** - Comma-separated, most relevant first
3. **Organize experience** - Use bullet points or clear structure
4. **Proofread content** - Check grammar and spelling
5. **Choose appropriate template** - Match your industry
6. **Select proper format** - PDF for final, DOCX for editing

### Customization:
- Change colors in `config.py` and `style.css`
- Add new templates by duplicating and modifying existing ones
- Enhance AI by editing `prompt_engine.py`
- Customize validation rules in `config.py`
