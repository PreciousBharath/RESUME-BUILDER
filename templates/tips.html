Resume Tips

Small improvements that make a big difference.

Tailor every resume

Match your summary and skills to the role you are applying for.

Use action verbs

Words like built, led, launched, and improved make your experience stronger.

Quantify wins

Include numbers, percentages, and results whenever possible.

Keep formatting clean

Readable fonts, consistent spacing, and clear sections help recruiters scan faster.
